OUR APPROACH

Don't just read
the claim. Trace it.

A useful check starts with a specific claim and follows it back to its original source. Dates, locations, and missing context matter as much as the headline.

  • Find the original source and understand what it actually says.
  • Compare independent, relevant reporting.
  • Look for evidence that supports and challenges the claim.
  • Explain what remains uncertain, without guessing.
